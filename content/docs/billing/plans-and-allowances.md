Docurama is self-serve from a single consultant up to a fifty-person company, with Enterprise handled by our sales team. This page explains the plans, what is included in all of them, and how allowances, seats and rollover are counted, so nothing on your invoice is a surprise.

Prices are in euro per month. Enterprise is quoted individually and is not self-serve: start the conversation with the lead form on the [enterprise page](/enterprise).

## What every plan includes

- **Unlimited signers and recipients.** Recipients never need a Docurama account and never consume a seat. An envelope addressed to one signer or to ten signers in routing order counts once against your allowance.
- **Simple and Advanced eSignatures** with PAdES sealing from B-B up to B-LTA, RFC 3161 timestamps, and honest conformance reporting. Docurama is not a qualified trust service provider and never claims qualified signatures — see [eIDAS and legal admissibility](/docs/compliance/eidas).
- **Envelope rollover on renewal**, capped at one monthly allowance (the math is below).
- **Committed-term discounts:** 3 months −5%, 6 months −10%, 12 months −15%. The monthly term is the default and carries no engagement.

Every new signup starts with a 14-day Starter trial, so you can evaluate with the Starter feature set before paying anything.

## The 35 sending days framing

On Solo and Starter, the monthly allowance is framed as **35 sending days a month — weekends are on us**. In practice this means your sending window never shrinks because a weekend falls inside it: the framing describes the period, while what is actually counted is envelopes, not days. Most small teams never reach the boundary.

## Seats and recipients

Seats are for workspace members — the people who prepare, send and manage envelopes in the portal. Everyone who signs is a recipient: recipients need neither an account nor a seat, and are unlimited on every plan. A consultant who sends on your behalf needs a seat; the five directors signing their NDA do not.

## Solo or Starter?

Solo and Starter have the **same 50-envelope monthly allowance and the same features**. The only difference is seats: Solo has one, Starter has three. Pick Solo if exactly one person will ever send documents; pick Starter as soon as a second or third teammate needs to send or manage envelopes. You can move between plans at any time from **Settings → Billing**.

## How rollover works

Unused envelopes carry forward when your subscription renews, **capped at one monthly allowance**:

- You are on Solo (50/month) and used 20 envelopes. 30 carry over, and the next period starts with an allowance of 80.
- You are on Solo and used none. 50 would carry over, but the cap is one monthly allowance, so the next period starts with 100 — not 150.

Rollover happens at renewal, never stacks beyond the cap, and the carried amount is visible in **Settings → Billing**, where the allowance bar shows the "+N carried over from last period".

## Changing plans

Plan and term changes are made from the plan and term picker in **Settings → Billing**. For how payment itself works — Stripe Checkout or bank transfer — see [Checkout and payments](/docs/billing/checkout-and-payments).
